# SUGI ASAYAKE Mongolia — Creative Direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Mizu Pop
Very clean Japanese retail minimalism, lifted by translucent color blocks, playful product photography, and a fresh water-blue foundation. The feeling is trustworthy and youthful without becoming childish.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Tokyo Night Market
A high-contrast editorial storefront with ink-black fields, vermilion accents, and electric cyan details. It feels energetic, urban, and fashion-forward, but can make a family health-and-beauty brand feel more nightlife than everyday care.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Asayake Color Field
A sunrise-inspired brand world: warm white, vermilion, persimmon, cobalt, and a small hit of chartreuse, arranged as oversized color fields with calm Japanese spacing. It is distinctive, optimistic, and premium while keeping the shopping information immediate.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Asayake Color Field

### Design Movement
Contemporary Japanese editorial retail design, informed by Swiss International Typographic Style and the visual rhythm of a Japanese morning market. The system balances precise grid logic with a few deliberately oversized, tactile shapes.

### Core Principles
1. **Trust, made visible:** official Japanese provenance and clear store information should be as prominent as the visual identity.
2. **Color as navigation:** color blocks separate brand story, assortment, and locations instead of relying on heavy borders or card stacks.
3. **Calm with one surprise:** most surfaces stay quiet and airy; each section earns one bright, memorable gesture.
4. **Useful before decorative:** every interaction should quickly answer where to find SUGI, what makes it different, or how to call.

### Color Philosophy
Warm milk-white and pale blue-gray create the quiet base of a clean drugstore. SUGI red is the trusted, ownable anchor, while persimmon and marigold introduce the emotional warmth of asayake (sunrise). Cobalt is reserved for wayfinding and call-to-action moments so the page stays colorful without becoming noisy.

### Layout Paradigm
An editorial vertical scroll with offset columns, oversized section numbers, and color fields that bleed past the content container. The hero uses a split asymmetry: a text-led left side and an illustrated product composition on the right. Store locations are presented as a route, not a generic three-card grid.

### Signature Elements
- A thin red “morning line” that travels through major section transitions.
- Oversized numerals and Japanese-inspired micro-labels for section navigation.
- Rounded color fields with one soft grain layer, paired with crisp black typography and small leaf/sun marks.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning a page or selecting a shelf label: quick, tactile, and informative. Navigation anchors scroll smoothly, cards lift a few pixels on hover, and the phone CTA is always one tap away on mobile.

### Animation
Use a 180–260ms ease-out for hover and button states. Reveal section content with small upward translations and opacity changes, staggered by 50ms. The morning line can draw in once as the page loads; floating color shapes should drift only subtly and stop under prefers-reduced-motion.

### Typography System
Use **DM Sans** for readable body copy and navigation, paired with **Space Grotesk** for bold display headlines and oversized numerals. Mongolian copy uses the same Latin-compatible family stack with generous line-height. Display text should be compact and confident; body text should never fall below 15px.

### Brand Essence
Official Japanese everyday care, brought closer to families in Ulaanbaatar through a trusted local store network. Personality: **precise, warm, optimistic**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and lightly poetic. CTAs are direct, location-aware, and never vague. Microcopy should sound like a knowledgeable store team, not a corporate brochure.

Example lines:
- “Япон чанар. Өдөр бүрийн ойрхон сонголт.”
- “Өөрт ойр салбараас эхлүүлээрэй.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied SUGI ASAYAKE master logo as the primary wordmark. Pair it with a small, text-free red sun/leaf symbol as a favicon and compact mobile brand mark; this symbol echoes the supplied logo without competing with it.

### Signature Brand Color
**SUGI Vermilion — #E31B3B.** It is unmistakably tied to the supplied logo, reads clearly on warm white, and carries the confidence of an official Japanese retail brand.

## Content and UX commitments

- Hero message: “ГЭР БҮЛИЙН ГИШҮҮН БҮРТ ЯПОН ЧАНАР” with official-product reassurance.
- Proof points: official subsidiary of Japan’s SUGI drug store, 800+ product varieties, 3 UB locations.
- Store finder: Russian Embassy west side, Ikh Nayad Plaza 1st floor, and Yarmag Food City 3rd floor.
- Conversion path: phone CTA to **9450-9911**, Storepay mention, and a simple “Find your store” anchor.
- The site is a promotional storefront, not a full checkout flow; it should make the next visit or call effortless.

## Style Decisions

- Keep near-black as one deliberate “one surprise” moment only; the dominant atmosphere stays warm milk-white and pale blue-gray with SUGI vermilion, marigold, persimmon, and restrained cobalt.
- Treat the thin vermilion morning line, oversized section numerals, and compact red sun/leaf mark as one connected wayfinding language across hero, proof points, assortment, store route, CTA, and footer.
- Use tactile Japanese everyday-care product imagery, shelf/store context, and Ulaanbaatar location reassurance in every key visual; avoid generic wellness advertising.

## Professional redesign decisions

- Replace cinematic chapter stacking with a clearer premium retail rhythm: split hero, proof strip, editorial story, selection feature, family-care panel, location finder, and direct contact CTA.
- Use large type only where it carries the brand promise; keep supporting copy, labels, and CTAs compact, calm, and easy to scan.
- Treat images as framed editorial assets with controlled cropping and purposeful captions, not full-screen backgrounds for every section.
- Keep the header quiet and paper-toned in its resting state, with a slim transition on scroll, so the site feels like a considered retail brand rather than a campaign template.
- Use one strong vermilion action color, one warm paper base, and dark ink typography. Marigold is a supporting signal only; avoid stacking multiple bright accents in the same component.
